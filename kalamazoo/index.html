How Kalamazoo Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

The age of your equipment matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like rising energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of the year.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected for a season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so help is one call away as soon as something seems off in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
